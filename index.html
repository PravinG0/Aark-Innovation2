Direct Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers regarding who hires us, vendor independence, and how we protect proprietary data.

Who typically engages AARK Innovations? +
Usually a VP of Sales, a COO, or a founder who realizes their team is spending too much time moving data between systems. They have revenue goals to hit and know that hiring more people to do manual data entry won't scale.
Do you only work with Oracle, AWS and specific vendors? +
No. We work across the modern stack. We frequently build on top of HubSpot, Salesforce, Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, connecting them via tools like Make, Zapier, or custom APIs. We focus on the business outcome, not forcing a specific vendor.
How fast do typical engagements move? +
Most initial engagements take between 4 and 8 weeks to get into production. We prefer shipping small, highly valuable automations quickly rather than spending six months building a monolithic system before you see value.
How do you protect proprietary data with enterprise AI? +
Yes. We never train public models on your proprietary data. We use enterprise-grade endpoints and architectures (like Retrieval-Augmented Generation) where your data stays strictly within your tenant boundary and respects your existing permission models.
